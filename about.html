About Viva Textorium

40 years of experience, one fabric at a time.

Viva Textorium LLC is a textile and fabric retailer in Abu Dhabi. Our shop is at Madinat Zayed Shopping Centre on Electra Street. It’s a place to open out a length of fabric, look at it closely and talk through what you’d like to make with it.


Over four decades in the textile trade, one question has stayed central to our work. How do you want the finished piece to look and feel? The answer guides everything from the first colour you pick up to the final fabric you choose.

If you have a sketch, we can talk about the shape you want to create. If you bring a favourite garment, you can show us the details you’d like to carry into a new piece. Sometimes pointing to a sleeve or a fold says more than knowing the name of the fabric.

What 40 Years of Experience Brings to Your Visit

An eye for the details that affect a design

A large print can look beautiful across the full width of a fabric. On a narrow sleeve or a small panel, you might see only part of the motif. Looking at the pattern in relation to your design helps you picture what you’ll actually wear.

Practical questions before you choose

Will the garment have gathers or pleats? Does it need lining? Has your tailor asked for a particular fabric width? These details make it easier to narrow down your options and know what to confirm before buying.

Room for personal preference

You may prefer a subtle change in texture over an exact colour match. Someone else may want a contrasting shade that picks up a single thread in an embroidered piece. The choice comes back to what looks right to you.

What to Expect in Store

  1. Show us your starting point

    Bring a photo, sample, sketch or existing garment. If you’re browsing, tell us which colours or textures have caught your eye.

  2. Compare fabrics side by side

    Look at your favourite options together. Hold each one up, check the scale of the pattern and see how the material falls.

  3. Check the practical details

    Ask about the price, fabric width and available length. If you’re working with a tailor, compare those details with their requirements before deciding.

Who We Can Help

Perhaps you’re buying fabric for the first time and your tailor has handed you a list. Perhaps you already have a precise shade in mind and need to see it in person.
