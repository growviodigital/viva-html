Ask About a Fabric

A photo and a few details help us give you a more useful answer. If you saw the fabric on our Instagram, send the post itself. If you’re matching something you own, a close-up taken in daylight can help us understand the shade you’re looking for.


You can start with a message like this:

Hello Viva Textorium, I’m looking for fabric similar to the one in this photo. I need around 4 metres in dark green for a dress. Could you let me know what’s available, the fabric width and the price per metre?
