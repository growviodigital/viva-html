Find Fabric for Your Project

For everyday wear

Think about a piece you reach for again and again. Is it soft against the skin, easy to move in or light enough to layer? Bring it along and use it as a starting point when choosing fabric for your next outfit.

For a special occasion

A celebration outfit often starts with a colour or a silhouette. Tell us what you have in mind, whether it’s a soft, flowing sleeve, a skirt with a fuller shape or a finish that catches the evening light.

To match something you own

Looking for fabric to go with an embroidered border, a dupatta or an existing garment? Bring the piece into the shop. Lay the fabrics alongside it and see which shade brings out the detail you love.

A Closer Look at Fabric

Colour & finish

Two fabrics in the same shade can look quite different. A smooth, lustrous surface reflects light, while a matte finish gives the colour a softer appearance. Open out the fabric to see how it looks across a larger area.

Texture & feel

Run your hand over the surface. If you’re choosing fabric for a collar, cuff or neckline, pay particular attention to how it feels where it will touch your skin.

Weight & drape

Lift a length of fabric and let it fall. Does it settle into gentle folds or hold a more defined shape? This simple check can help you picture a finished sleeve, pleat or hem.

Before You Make the Trip

Can I check a fabric’s availability first?

Yes. Message us with a reference photo or a short description. Include your preferred colour and the number of metres you need so we can check whether there’s enough for your project.

What if I don’t know the fabric name?

Describe what you can see or feel. Words like “slightly shiny,” “textured” or “falls in soft folds” give us a place to start. A photo of the full garment and a close-up of the fabric can also help.

What should I bring if my tailor is making the outfit?

Bring the design and any measurements or fabric requirements your tailor has given you. If they’ve specified a width as well as a length, bring both details along.
